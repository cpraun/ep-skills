Please assess whether the following divisional claim complies with Art. 76(1) EPC.

**Divisional — claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising a double-walled vessel whose outer wall is made
   of titanium and bears a ceramic coating.

**Earlier (parent) application as filed — relevant passages**

[0030] The outer wall may be made of stainless steel, aluminium, or titanium.

[0031] The outer wall may bear a coating selected from PTFE, ceramic, or powder
paint.

The parent nowhere links a particular wall material to a particular coating; there
is no pointer to titanium together with a ceramic coating.
