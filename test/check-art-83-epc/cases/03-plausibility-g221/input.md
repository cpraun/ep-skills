Please assess the following claim for sufficiency of disclosure under Art. 83 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. Use of a coating of compound X on the outer wall of a beverage container for
   reducing the heat loss of the container by at least 50 %.

**Description (application as filed, relevant passages)**

[0011] It has surprisingly been found that a coating of compound X reduces the
heat loss of the container by at least 50 %.

The application contains no measurements, no comparative data, no examples, and no
explanation of a mechanism. The 50 % reduction is asserted but not otherwise
supported.
