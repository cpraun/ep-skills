Please assess the following claim for sufficiency of disclosure under Art. 83 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising a double-walled vessel having a vacuum with a
   residual pressure of at most 1 × 10⁻⁹ mbar between the walls.

**Description (application as filed, relevant passages)**

[0022] The space between the walls is evacuated using a standard rotary vane pump
during assembly, reaching a residual pressure of about 1 × 10⁻³ mbar.

The application describes no ultra-high-vacuum equipment, no getter, and no
sealing technique capable of reaching 10⁻⁹ mbar, and gives no other route to the
claimed pressure.
