Please assess the following claim for sufficiency of disclosure under Art. 83 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising a double-walled vessel whose outer wall bears a
   coating selected from any thermally insulating polymer.

**Description (application as filed, relevant passages)**

[0012] The outer wall bears a thermally insulating coating that reduces heat loss.

[0030] In the only worked example, the coating is polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE),
applied by spray coating and cured at 380 °C. No other polymer is exemplified or
described, and the application gives no guidance on selecting among other
thermally insulating polymers or on the process conditions they would require.
