Please assess the following claim for sufficiency of disclosure under Art. 83 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising an outer coating that inhibits condensation on
   the outer surface of the container.

**Description (application as filed, relevant passages)**

[0014] The outer surface carries a coating that inhibits condensation.

The application defines the coating purely by the function it performs. It gives
no list of suitable coating materials, no selection criteria, and no test for
identifying which coatings inhibit condensation. The skilled person would have to
run a research programme to find coatings meeting the functional definition.
