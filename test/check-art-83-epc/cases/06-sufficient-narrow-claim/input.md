Please assess the following claim for sufficiency of disclosure under Art. 83 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising a double-walled stainless-steel vessel with a
   vacuum between the walls and a polypropylene lid, constructed as in Example 1.

**Description (application as filed, relevant passages)**

[0040] Example 1. An inner and an outer wall, each of 0.3 mm 304 stainless steel,
are joined at the rim by laser welding to define a 2 mm gap. The gap is evacuated
to about 1 × 10⁻³ mbar with a rotary vane pump and sealed. A lid is injection-
moulded from polypropylene and threaded onto the mouth. The complete assembly and
all dimensions, materials, and process steps are given in [0040]–[0046].
