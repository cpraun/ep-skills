Please assess the following amendment for compliance with Art. 123(2) EPC.

**Amendment under examination**

Claim 1 is amended to add:
"… wherein the outer wall is made of titanium and bears a ceramic coating."

**Application as originally filed (relevant passages)**

[0030] The outer wall may be made of stainless steel, aluminium, or titanium.

[0031] The outer wall may bear a coating selected from PTFE, ceramic, or powder
paint.

The application nowhere links a particular wall material to a particular coating;
there is no statement pointing to titanium together with a ceramic coating.
