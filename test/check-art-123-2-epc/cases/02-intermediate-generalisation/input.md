**Amendment under examination**

Claim 1 is amended to add:

**Application as originally filed (relevant passages)**

[0009] The invention relates to a beverage container comprising a double-walled
vessel with a vacuum between the walls, closed by a lid.

[0020] In one embodiment, the lid is moulded from polypropylene and carries a
silicone sealing ring together with a stainless-steel latch, the sealing ring and
the latch cooperating to close and seal the drinking aperture.

The silicone sealing ring is mentioned nowhere else in the application; it appears
only in the [0020] embodiment, described together with the polypropylene lid and
the stainless-steel latch.
