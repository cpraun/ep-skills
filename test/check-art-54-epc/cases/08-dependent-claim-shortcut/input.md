Please assess the novelty of claims 1 and 2 over D1 under Art. 54 EPC.

**Claims**

1. A beverage container comprising:
   (a) a double-walled vessel defining an inner chamber;
   (b) a vacuum between the two walls;
   (c) a lid having a resealable drinking aperture; and
   (d) a thermochromic indicator on the outer wall that changes colour above 60 °C.

2. The beverage container of claim 1, wherein the thermochromic indicator is a
   printed adhesive label.

**D1 — WO 2015/000000 A1**

[0018] The insulated cup comprises an inner wall and an outer wall that together
define a hollow body enclosing a chamber for a drink.

[0019] The space between the walls is evacuated to a vacuum.

[0020] A screw-on lid carries a sliding closure over a drinking opening that can
be resealed after use.

D1 does not mention any indicator, ink, label, or colour change.
