Please assess the novelty of claim 1 over D1 under Art. 54 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising a double-walled vacuum vessel and a
   thermochromic indicator on the outer wall that changes colour within the
   range of 62 °C to 63 °C.

**D1 — WO 2015/000000 A1**

[0027] The container has a double-walled vacuum body and a thermochromic
indicator on its outer wall. The transition temperature of the indicator may be
selected anywhere in the range of 40 °C to 90 °C depending on the intended
drink.

[0028] In a worked example, the indicator is formulated to change colour at
62.5 °C, which the inventors found suitable for coffee.
