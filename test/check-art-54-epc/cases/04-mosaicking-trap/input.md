Please assess the novelty of claim 1 over D1 under Art. 54 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising:
   (a) a double-walled vessel defining an inner chamber;
   (b) a vacuum between the two walls;
   (c) a lid having a resealable drinking aperture; and
   (d) a thermochromic indicator on the outer wall that changes colour above 60 °C.

**D1 — WO 2015/000000 A1**

D1 describes two separate and unrelated products.

*First embodiment (Figures 1–3), "the travel mug":*

[0040] The travel mug has a double-walled body with an evacuated (vacuum) space
between the inner and outer walls, the inner wall defining a chamber for the
drink. A screw-on lid carries a sliding closure over a drinking opening that can
be resealed after use. The travel mug has a plain, unprinted outer wall.

*Second embodiment (Figures 4–5), "the disposable paper cup":*

[0055] The disposable paper cup is a single-walled cup wound from paperboard,
with no vacuum and no lid. A band of thermochromic ink around its outer surface
turns red above 60 °C to indicate a hot drink.

D1 contains no statement, cross-reference, or suggestion that features of the
travel mug and features of the disposable paper cup could be combined; the two
embodiments are presented as distinct products.
