Please assess the novelty of claim 1 over D1 under Art. 54 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising:
   (a) a double-walled vessel defining an inner chamber;
   (b) a vacuum between the two walls;
   (c) a lid having a resealable drinking aperture; and
   (d) a thermochromic indicator on the outer wall that changes colour above 60 °C.

**D1 — WO 2015/000000 A1**

[0031] The device is built around a standard vacuum flask body. A screw-on lid
closes the top and carries a sliding closure over a drinking opening, allowing
the opening to be resealed after use.

[0032] A band of thermochromic ink around the outer surface turns red above
60 °C to warn that the contents are hot.

D1 gives no further constructional detail of the "vacuum flask body" beyond the
term itself. It does not use the words "double wall", "two walls", or "inner
chamber", and does not separately state that a vacuum is present.
