Please assess the novelty of claim 1 over D1 under Art. 54 EPC.

**Claim 1**

1. A beverage container comprising:
   (a) a double-walled vessel defining an inner chamber;
   (b) a vacuum between the two walls;
   (c) a lid having a resealable drinking aperture; and
   (d) a thermochromic indicator on the outer wall that changes colour above 60 °C.

**D1 — WO 2015/000000 A1**

[0018] The insulated cup comprises an inner wall and an outer wall that together
define a hollow body, the inner wall enclosing a chamber for holding a drink.

[0019] The space between the inner and outer walls is evacuated to a vacuum in
order to reduce heat transfer.

[0020] A screw-on lid closes the top of the cup. The lid carries a sliding
closure over a drinking opening, so that the opening can be resealed after use.

[0021] A band of thermochromic ink is printed around the outer wall. The ink is
colourless below 60 °C and turns red above 60 °C, warning the user that the
contents are hot. All of the above features are shown together in Figure 1.
