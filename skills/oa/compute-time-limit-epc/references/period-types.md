# Rules for specific period types

Part of the `compute-time-limit-epc` skill. Read the section for the event identified in Step 1. Verbatim Rule text is in `legal-basis-reference.md`.

## Contents
- Rule 132 EPC — periods set by the EPO, extension
- Rule 116(1) EPC — final date before oral proceedings
- Art. 121 + Rule 135 EPC — further processing

## Rule 132 EPC — periods set by the EPO

- Normally two to four months; up to six months in exceptional cases. Typical: four months for substantive communications (Art. 94(3), R. 70a(2), opposition on substance); two months for formal matters; six months for Euro-PCT R. 161/162. Always use what the communication sets.
- **Extension:** requested in writing **before expiry**; the extended period runs **from the start of the original period**. In examination, one extension to a total of six months is normally granted without reasons (Guidelines E-VIII, 1.6.1); beyond that only for substantiated exceptional circumstances (not leave or workload). In opposition, only in exceptional, substantiated cases.
- **Not available** for periods fixed by the EPC itself (e.g., Art. 87 priority, Art. 99 opposition, Art. 108 appeal) or for the R. 116(1) final date.

## Rule 116(1) EPC — final date for written submissions

Set in the summons itself: report it as set, do not compute it. Not extendable under R. 132 (R. 116(1), third sentence). Typically one month before oral proceedings in examination, two months in opposition. R. 134(1) applies if it falls on a closing day. Late facts and evidence need not be considered unless admitted because the subject of the proceedings has changed.

## Art. 121 + Rule 135 EPC — further processing

- **R. 135(1):** pay the fee and complete the omitted act **within two months** of the communication of the missed time limit or loss of rights (typically the R. 112(1) notification). This period follows R. 131(2) and R. 126(2)/127(2) like any notified period — no 10-day add-on.
- **R. 135(2) exclusions:** the periods in Art. 121(4) (including Art. 108) and R. 6(1), R. 16(1)(a), R. 31(2), R. 36(2), R. 40(3), R. 51(2)–(5), R. 52(2)–(3), R. 55, R. 56, R. 58, R. 59, R. 62a, R. 63, R. 64, R. 112(2), R. 164(1)–(2), and the further-processing period itself.

## Art. 122 + Rule 136 EPC — re-establishment of rights

- Available where further processing is not, subject to all due care. Where further processing is available, re-establishment can only be requested in respect of the further-processing period (R. 136(3)).
- **R. 136(1):** request in writing within **two months of removal of the cause** of non-compliance, but **not later than one year after expiry** of the unobserved period. For the priority period, see the special case in R. 136(1), second sentence.
- **R. 136(2):** state the grounds and facts, complete the omitted act within the same two months, and pay the fee.

## Art. 108 EPC — appeal

- **Notice of appeal and appeal fee:** two months from notification of the decision.
- **Statement of grounds:** four months from notification.
- Fixed by the EPC: no R. 132 extension, no further processing (Art. 121(4)). R. 134(1) and the R. 126(2) 7-day safeguard apply normally. Re-establishment under Art. 122 remains available.
