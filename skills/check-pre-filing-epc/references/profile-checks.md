# Additional checks by draft profile

Part of the `check-pre-filing-epc` skill. Apply only the sections that match the profile established in Step 3.

## By drafting origin

- *US style*: claims-oriented boilerplate ("intended to cover all modifications", "claims, if any", definitions of "coupled", "about"), incorporation by reference, "non-transitory" medium wording, means-plus-function claims, Letter-size pages, "comprising"/"consisting of" alignment with the claims, background without problem statement.
- *DE/European style*: "insbesondere"/"vorzugsweise" in independent claims, "erfindungsgemäß" statements limiting the scope, two-part form already present and correct, reference signs in claims.
- *Translations (e.g. JP/CN/KR)*: inconsistent translation of the same term, literal translations that change meaning, units and number formats, unclear "unit"/"section" terminology.
- *Inventor/in-house drafts*: missing generalisation, missing fallback layers, statements of essentiality, commercial or marketing language, missing problem/effect statements.

## By technical field

- *CII/AI*: COMVIK two-hurdle test (Art. 52(2),(3)); identify features at risk of being non-technical and propose technical fallbacks; claim set method + apparatus + computer program/medium (Guidelines F-IV 3.9); for AI, disclosure of model and training data (G-II 3.3.1, T 161/18).
- *Chemistry/pharma/materials*: ranges and end points, selection inventions, Markush groups, measuring methods for parameters (F-IV 4.11), purpose-limited product claims (Art. 54(4),(5)), plausibility of effects for later reliance (G 2/21), examples supporting the claimed breadth.
- *Medical*: exclusions under Art. 53(c) (methods of treatment, surgery, diagnosis); use or product claims instead.
- *Biotech*: Art. 53(b), Rule 28 exclusions; sequence listings in WIPO ST.26 (Rule 30); deposit of biological material (Rule 31).
- *Mechanical/electrical*: functional features with sufficient structure, consistency of reference signs across figures, intermediate generalisations to avoid later (Art. 123(2)).
