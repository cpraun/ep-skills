# Special cases in the Art. 76(1) combination check

Part of the `check-art-76-1-epc` skill. Read the section that applies when the divisional claim contains such a feature or the parent has such an origin.

## Numerical ranges

A range in the divisional that is narrower than, or shifted from, a range in the parent is admissible only if the new range is itself disclosed in the parent: explicitly, as a preferred sub-range, or as directly and unambiguously derivable from examples plus general description (T 2/81, T 201/83). For combined end-points: a preferred range plus an adjoining part-range of the general range is disclosed (T 2/81); a range from the lower limit of the general range and the lower limit of the preferred range, excluding the preferred range, is not (T 1170/02 — a divisional case).

## Generalisation

A specific value, material or embodiment of the parent recast as a broader term is admissible only if the broader concept itself is disclosed in the parent, typically in a general passage of the description.

## Disclaimers

A disclaimer present in the divisional but not in the parent is either disclosed (assessed under G 2/10) or undisclosed (admissible only under the strict conditions of G 1/03 / G 2/03 and G 1/16, by reference to prior art). Classify it, apply the corresponding test in the Assessment column, and do not draft alternative wording.

## Euro-PCT parent

The "earlier application as filed" is the international application as filed (Art. 153(2) EPC). Use it as the basis document.
